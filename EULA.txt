WHAT THE SOFTWARE DOES
----------------------
ClearWin adjusts Windows system settings automatically: process priorities,
network parameters, power plans, services and other performance-related
options. It includes backups, restore points and an emergency kill switch,
but no safety mechanism can be guaranteed to reverse every change in every
environment.
